DIGITAL AND EMAIL SPEND FILES - SPECIFICATION (v1.4)

1. Overview
Each file contains one row per calendar month with total spend in USD thousands.

2. Fields
month (date, YYYY-MM-DD): first day of the calendar month the spend relates to.
spend_usd_k (decimal): total platform spend for the whole month, invoiced monthly.

3. Notes
Daily delivery is not available. Spend within a month should be treated as total only; no weekly phasing is provided.
