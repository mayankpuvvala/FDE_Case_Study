BRAND SALES FILE - SPECIFICATION (v3.0)

1. Overview
Net sales in USD thousands, aggregated by calendar month from the first to the last day of the month.

2. Fields
month (date, YYYY-MM-DD): first day of the calendar month.
sales_usd_k (decimal): net sales for the month after returns and rebates.

3. Notes
Months contain a different number of days (28 to 31); no day-count normalisation is applied to the values.
