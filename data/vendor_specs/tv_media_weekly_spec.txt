TV MEDIA SPEND FILE - VENDOR SPECIFICATION (v2.3)

1. Overview
Spend is reported by media-buying week. Each row is one week of gross TV spend in USD thousands.

2. Fields
week_commencing (date, YYYY-MM-DD): the Monday on which the buying week commences.
spend_usd_k (decimal): gross spend booked against that week, before agency commission.

3. Calendar
Weeks always run Monday to Sunday and follow the broadcast calendar. Weeks are grouped into 4-4-5 week quarters for billing.
Buying weeks do not align with calendar months; a week may straddle two months.

4. Delivery
The file is refreshed every Monday and contains all weeks to date. Late invoices may restate prior weeks.
