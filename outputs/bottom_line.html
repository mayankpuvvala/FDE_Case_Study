The Two Calendars · bottom line

Should we move 15% of TV spend to digital + email?

No. The data do not support the shift. The naive (week → month of its Monday) calendar shows a small gain, but it is an artefact of the calendar error. With the corrected day-level calendar there is no gain in any scenario, and the true effect was negative in every simulated world.

If there is a gain, how much?

  • Naive calendar, steady TV: +0.52% of sales (upper bound +1.4%). This is the only place a gain appears, and it is not real.
  • Corrected calendar: no gain. Best estimate is -0.12% (steady TV) and -0.82% (pulsed TV).
  • Truth: -0.62% and -0.53%. Moving the spend costs roughly half a percent of sales.
  • False-positive rate: the naive calendar showed a confident gain in 33% of 12 steady-TV worlds; the corrected one in 0%.

Caveats

  • With steady TV the corrected result is statistically indistinguishable from zero: the honest answer there is "cannot tell", not "the shift is bad".
  • Only 48 monthly points; intervals are optimistic (the 90% interval caught the true TV share only 25% of the time in the pulsed case).
