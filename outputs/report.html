01What this shows

The error is realNaive aggregation books whole weeks to one month. A typical month is off by about 12%; total spend is unchanged.
It matters most when TV is steadySpurious month-to-month swings make TV look weak, which is exactly the signal behind “shift to digital”.
The shift is not demonstratedThe correct calendar never showed a confident gain in any world, and the true effect was negative in every one.

04Honest limits

  • The data are synthetic and the true effects (TV 18%, digital 14%, email 6%) are our design. This validates a method and a mechanism, not the real brand's numbers.
  • Only 48 monthly points. The bootstrap captures sampling noise but not model misspecification (fixed saturation, monthly adstock against a daily truth), so intervals are optimistic.
  • Spend is assumed even within a week; real phasing may differ.
  • With steady TV the correct series barely varies, so TV is weakly identified: the honest answer there is “cannot tell”, not “the shift is bad”.
